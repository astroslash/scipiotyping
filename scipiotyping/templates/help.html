Help

How to practice

Choose a passage, place your fingers comfortably, and begin. Look at the passage rather than your hands. Slow down when accuracy falls.

Scores

Raw WPM counts every five typed characters as one word. Adjusted WPM counts only characters that align correctly with the passage. Accuracy uses aligned matches, substitutions, insertions, deletions, and reversed pairs, so one missing letter or space does not make the rest of the passage wrong.

Finishing

ScipioTyping detects when the ending has been reached, even if an earlier character was missed. A Finish and score button appears after most of the passage has been typed as a fallback.

Troubleshooting

If the page does not open, confirm the ScipioTyping PowerShell window is still running. Your progress remains in the local database after the browser closes.
