# ScipioTyping 1.4 content plan and editorial record

Version 1.4 doubles the built-in library from 60 to 120 passages without adding
new subject names or altering released v1.3 passage objects. Each of the ten
subjects receives six additions. The new difficulty distribution is Level 2: 5,
Level 3: 15, Level 4: 25, and Level 5: 15.

## Release gates

- Prose is newly written; modern source wording and full poems are not copied.
- Myth is labeled as myth, disputed claims are qualified, and historical sources
  are distinguished from complete accounts of the past.
- War content explains logistics, civilians, uncertainty, and consequences
  without graphic detail or triumphalism.
- Every addition includes context, two vocabulary explanations, original-rights
  metadata, a review date, and a structured HTTPS reference.
- New passages remain within their Level 2–5 word band. Level 1 remains reserved
  for focused typing drills.
- Automated validation, inventory generation, release checks, and the real Edge
  workflow must pass before tagging the release.
