# ScipioTyping 1.10.0

Version 1.10.0 adds twenty original long-form typing passages for Middle School.
Each of the ten advanced subjects receives one Level 4 and one Level 5 passage,
with Grade 7–8 language and 175–215 words per exercise. The Elementary library
remains unchanged at thirty short Grade 3 passages.

The new lessons cover Yorktown, D-Day planning, chess clocks and notation,
Roman aqueducts, Athenian ostracism, the Iliad and Odyssey, Greek and Roman myth,
wartime communication, Lincoln, Eleanor Roosevelt, recursion, graph theory,
public-domain poetry, the Swahili coast, and Meiji Japan. Every passage is
original prose with reviewed metadata and a structured HTTPS reference.

## Acceptance

- The complete library contains 170 passages: 140 Middle School and 30 Elementary.
- Every advanced subject contains fourteen passages.
- The twenty additions are explicitly restricted to Middle School.
- Each addition is between 175 and 215 words and at least 1,100 characters.
- Content validation, automated tests, release checks, and browser smoke checks pass.
